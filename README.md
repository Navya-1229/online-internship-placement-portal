# Online Internship & Placement Preparation Portal

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Online Internship & Placement Preparation Portal** is a web-based platform designed to help students discover internship opportunities and prepare for campus placements.

The platform provides a centralized system where students can explore internships, submit applications, practice aptitude tests, monitor their preparation progress, and analyze their performance.

Recruiters can manage internship opportunities and student applications, while administrators oversee users, internships, tests, and platform activities.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Provide students with a centralized platform for internship opportunities.
* Simplify the internship application and tracking process.
* Support placement preparation through online aptitude tests.
* Monitor student preparation progress and performance.
* Enable recruiters to manage internship postings and applications efficiently.
* Provide administrators with tools for managing and monitoring the platform.
